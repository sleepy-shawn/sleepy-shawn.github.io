CLIP#

More complex positional encoding.

Attention pooling is used in the ResNet version of CLIP.

There is another ViT version.

Reproduced in OpenCLIP (using the LAION-5B dataset, which used CLIP for filtering).

Data processing#

Images come in all resolutions (arbitrary W × H).

  • Resize using bicubic interpolation so the shorter side is 336 pixels.
  • Center crop (cuts off the borders to get 336 × 336).

Vision encoder: ResNet or ViT.

Text encoder:

  • GPT-2 Transformer (63M parameters, 12 layers).
  • Encode [BOS] … [EOS] and return the [EOS] activation at the highest layer.

Ablation:

  • Alternative: predict text from images directly.
  • Much less compute-efficient compared to CLIP-style ranking.

SigLIP#

SigLIP: binary classification for (text, image) pairs — aligned or not?

Data:

  • WebLI dataset: O(billion) (image, text) pairs.
  • Chen+ 2022
  • Scraped from the Internet.
  • Use automatic OCR to extract text from images.
  • Keep the highest-quality 10%.
  • Supports 100 languages.

LLaVA#

Vision encoder: CLIP.

Text decoder: Vicuna (LLaMA fine-tuned on ShareGPT conversations).

Data:

  • MS COCO has images annotated with bounding boxes and Mechanical Turk captions.
  • Prompt GPT-4 with captions or detected objects and generate questions or conversations.
  • Pair the generations with the original images.
  • 158K examples.

Training:

  • Stage 1 (alignment): freeze the vision encoder and language model, and only train W.
  • Stage 2 (fine-tuning): freeze the vision encoder and train W and the language model.

LLaVA OneVision#

Vision encoder: SigLIP (use grid features before and after the last Transformer layer).

Text decoder: Qwen-2 72B.

Projector: 2-layer MLP.

AnyRes, introduced in LLaVA 1.5.

Break up the image into a × b pieces (matching the resolution of the vision encoder), encode them, and concatenate them.

If there are too many tokens (the original image has too high a resolution), then use bilinear interpolation.

Transfer between modalities:

  • Single-image data for diagrams and charts, but generalize to multiple images.
  • OCR on single-image data and relational reasoning from multi-image data generalize to GUI-based agents.
  • Visual prompting (circles) in single images generalizes to videos.

Standard VLM template: vision encoder + projector + LM.

Qwen#

Qwen2-VL: MRoPE.

Qwen3-VL: Qwen-3 models, SigLIP2. Interleaved MRoPE: distribute all axes (temporal, width, height) to low- and high-frequency bands.

The adapter is DeepStack: cross-layer fusion to inject visual information into multiple layers.

Chameleon#

Chameleon: map everything into discrete tokens.

Advantage: can analyze and generate images in a uniform way.

Vision encoder: VQ-VAE.

Training stability:

Text tokens have low entropy, while image tokens have high entropy, leading to norm growth and logit drift problems.

Fixes: QK norm and z-loss regularization.

Elegant but poor performance.
