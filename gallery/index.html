Advice for the Next Five Years of a PhD

Fable 5.2 · Saved November 10, 2026

Read the transcript

Prompt: your advice for the next 5 years PhD student, no explanations

  1. Pick the advisor, not the project.
  2. Write every day, even badly.
  3. Publish something small early.
  4. Read papers outside your subfield.
  5. Learn to use AI tools well, and learn what they get wrong.
  6. Build one skill that transfers outside academia.
  7. Keep a research log.
  8. Go to conferences and talk to people.
  9. Say no to most side projects.
  10. Ask for feedback before you feel ready.
  11. Protect your sleep, exercise, and friendships.
  12. Take real vacations.
  13. Track your finances and apply for every fellowship.
  14. Decide by year 3 whether you want academia.
  15. Done beats perfect, so finish.
